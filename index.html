Decision guide

Use a blended response when one intervention cannot close the gap in time.
Allocate internally

Best when qualified capacity exists and reassignment does not create a more serious shortage elsewhere.

Train / upskill

Best when demand is recurring and employees have a realistic learning pathway before the required date.

Hire / contract

Best when the skill is specialised, urgent, persistently scarce, and cannot be developed internally in time.

How to interpret the model

Internal allocation

Typically faster and less expensive, but only if the employee has real capacity and reassignment does not move the gap elsewhere.

Training

Builds durable capability, but effectiveness depends on starting proficiency, coaching, certification, and lead time.

Hiring

Can close specialist gaps faster than training, but recruiting lead time, onboarding, and total employment cost still matter.
